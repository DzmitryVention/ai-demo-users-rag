Several weight limits apply to your vehicle. They are shown on the manufacturer's plate and in the registration document:

- **Maximum permitted weight** of the loaded vehicle;
- **Maximum train weight** — the vehicle plus a trailer;
- **Maximum axle loads**;
- **Maximum roof load** and **tow-ball load**, given in the official manual.

The vehicle's payload is the maximum permitted weight minus the weight of the empty vehicle, and it includes passengers, luggage, accessories and the tow-ball load.

> **WARNING:** Never exceed the permitted weights. Overloading affects braking, steering and tyre safety and may be illegal.

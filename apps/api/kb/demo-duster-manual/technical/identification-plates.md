The manufacturer's plate, usually on a door pillar or under the bonnet, shows the VIN, the vehicle type approval and the permitted weights.

The exact technical data for your vehicle — engine, power, dimensions, capacities, fluid grades and weights — depend on the version and are given in:

- the vehicle registration document;
- the manufacturer's plate;
- the official owner's manual and the service record.

This demo guide deliberately does not list figures, so that you always use the official values for your exact vehicle.

> **NOTE:** When in doubt about a specification, ask an authorised workshop with your VIN at hand.

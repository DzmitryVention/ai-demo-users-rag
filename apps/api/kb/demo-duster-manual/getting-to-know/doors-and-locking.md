Press the lock button on the remote to lock all doors and the tailgate; press the unlock button to open them. On versions with a hands-free card, the doors can also be unlocked by pressing the button on the door handle while the card is on you.

From inside, the central locking switch on the dashboard locks or unlocks all doors at once. On many versions the doors lock automatically once the vehicle starts moving.

**Child safety lock.** The rear doors have a child safety lock on the edge of the door. When it is engaged, the door cannot be opened from inside.

> **WARNING:** Always engage the rear child safety locks when children travel in the back.

> **WARNING:** Do not drive with the doors locked from outside with nobody able to unlock them; it could delay rescuers in an emergency.

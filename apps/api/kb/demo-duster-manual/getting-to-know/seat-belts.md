Every occupant must wear a seat belt on every journey, including short ones.

- Sit upright with your back against the backrest.
- The diagonal strap should cross the middle of your shoulder and chest, never your neck.
- The lap strap should sit low across the hips, not across the stomach.
- Remove slack by pulling the strap gently towards the retractor.

A reminder light and buzzer warn you when a front belt is not fastened while driving.

> **WARNING:** A seat belt that has been worn during a serious accident must be replaced, even if it looks undamaged. Have the belts and their anchorages checked by a qualified workshop.

> **WARNING:** Never use one belt for two people, and never hold a child on your lap.

Children must travel in a child seat suitable for their size and weight, approved to the current regulations.

**ISOFIX.** Rear outer seats are usually fitted with ISOFIX anchor points between the backrest and the cushion. ISOFIX child seats click directly onto these points, which makes installation quicker and more reliable. Many seats also use a top tether or support leg — follow the child seat maker's instructions.

**Front passenger seat.** Rear-facing child seats must never be installed on the front passenger seat unless the front passenger airbag has been deactivated. Check the airbag status indicator before every journey.

> **WARNING:** A rear-facing child seat in front of an active passenger airbag can cause serious injury or death to the child if the airbag deploys.

> **NOTE:** The rear seat is the safest place for children under 12 years old.

The instrument panel in front of the driver shows the speedometer, rev counter (on some versions), fuel gauge, warning lights and a display for the trip computer.

The **trip computer** can show the distance travelled, average fuel consumption, range remaining and outside temperature. Cycle through the screens with the button on the steering wheel or the stalk.

**Service reminder.** The display reminds you when a service is due, based on distance and time. When the reminder appears, book an appointment with a workshop.

Warning lights are explained on a separate page. A light that comes on briefly when the engine starts and then goes out is a normal self-test.

> **NOTE:** Messages on the display can include a symbol and short text. If a message is not clear, note its exact text before contacting support.

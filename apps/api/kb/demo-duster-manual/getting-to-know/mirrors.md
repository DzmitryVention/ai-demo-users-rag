Adjust the mirrors after adjusting your seat.

- **Interior mirror:** frame the whole rear window. Many versions have a night position or an automatic anti-glare function.
- **Door mirrors:** adjust them electrically with the control on the driver's door, or manually on some versions. You should just see the side of your vehicle in the inner edge of each mirror.
- **Folding:** door mirrors can be folded when parking in narrow spaces.

Door mirrors are convex: objects appear further away than they really are.

> **NOTE:** On versions with heated door mirrors, the heating works together with the rear screen demister.

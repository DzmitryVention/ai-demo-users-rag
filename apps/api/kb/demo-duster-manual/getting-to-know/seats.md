A correct driving position reduces fatigue and helps the seat belt and airbags protect you.

1. Move the seat forward or back so that you can press the clutch or brake pedal fully while your knee stays slightly bent.
2. Adjust the backrest so that, with your back against it, your wrists rest on the top of the steering wheel with arms extended.
3. Adjust the seat height, where fitted, so that you have a good view of the road and the instruments.
4. Adjust the head restraint so that its top is level with the top of your head.

> **WARNING:** Never adjust the driver's seat while driving. The seat could move suddenly and you could lose control of the vehicle.

> **WARNING:** Do not recline the backrest too far while travelling; the seat belt cannot protect you correctly.

Airbags work together with the seat belts. Depending on the version, the vehicle has front airbags for the driver and passenger, side airbags and curtain airbags.

An airbag inflates in a fraction of a second during certain collisions and then deflates. It is designed to protect an occupant who is correctly seated and wearing a seat belt.

If the airbag warning light stays on after starting the engine, or comes on while driving, the system may not work in an accident.

> **WARNING:** Do not place objects, stickers or covers on the steering wheel, the dashboard or the seat sides where airbags are located.

> **WARNING:** Any work on the airbag system, steering wheel, dashboard or front seats must be carried out by a qualified workshop. If the airbag warning light stays on, have the system checked as soon as possible.

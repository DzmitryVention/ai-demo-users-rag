Use the controls on the centre console to set temperature, fan speed and air distribution. On versions with automatic climate control, choose a temperature and press AUTO: the system manages the fan and distribution for you.

**Air conditioning (A/C).** Switch it on to cool and dry the air. Running it for a few minutes regularly, even in winter, keeps the system in good condition.

**Recirculation.** Recirculating the cabin air helps to block outside smells and dust for short periods. Do not leave it on for long, because the windows can mist up and the air becomes stale.

> **NOTE:** It is normal for water to drip under the vehicle after using the air conditioning; it is condensation.

> **NOTE:** If the air conditioning no longer cools or smells unpleasant, have it checked by a workshop. The cabin filter is replaced during servicing.

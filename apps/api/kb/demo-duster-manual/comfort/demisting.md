For quick demisting of the windscreen, select the windscreen demisting mode, set the fan to maximum and the temperature to warm. Switching on the air conditioning dries the air and clears the windows faster.

The **heated rear screen** button also heats the door mirrors on versions so equipped. It switches off automatically after a few minutes.

In cold weather, clear all snow and ice from the windows, mirrors, lights and roof before driving.

> **WARNING:** Never drive with misted or icy windows. You must have a clear view all around the vehicle.

> **NOTE:** Do not use hot water to defrost the windscreen; it can crack the glass.

The rear seat backrests can be folded down, fully or partly depending on the version, to increase the boot space. Make sure the backrests lock securely when you raise them again.

**Loading the boot.** Place heavy objects against the rear seat backrest and as low as possible. Secure loads so that they cannot move when braking.

**Roof bars.** Use only roof bars approved for your vehicle and respect the maximum roof load given in the official manual. Spread the load evenly and secure it firmly.

> **WARNING:** Unsecured objects become dangerous projectiles in a sudden stop. Never place objects on the parcel shelf or dashboard.

> **NOTE:** A loaded roof rack changes the vehicle's behaviour in crosswinds and increases fuel consumption; adapt your speed.

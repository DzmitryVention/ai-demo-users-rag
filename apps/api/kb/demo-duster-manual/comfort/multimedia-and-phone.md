Depending on the version, the multimedia system provides radio, navigation, a phone hands-free function and smartphone replication.

**Pairing a phone by Bluetooth:**

1. With the vehicle stationary, open the phone menu on the multimedia screen and choose to add a device.
2. Enable Bluetooth on your phone and select the vehicle name in the list.
3. Confirm the code shown on both screens.

Once paired, the phone connects automatically when you get in. Smartphone replication usually requires a USB cable or a compatible wireless connection.

> **WARNING:** Do not handle the multimedia system or your phone while driving. Make settings only when the vehicle is stationary.

> **NOTE:** Before selling the vehicle, delete your paired phones and personal data from the system.

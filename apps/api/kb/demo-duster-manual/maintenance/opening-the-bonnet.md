title: Opening the bonnet
ordinal: 1
links: [maintenance-engine-oil, maintenance-coolant, maintenance-battery]
---

1. Pull the bonnet release handle located under the dashboard on the driver's side.
2. At the front of the vehicle, find the safety catch under the front edge of the bonnet and push or lift it to release the bonnet.
3. Raise the bonnet and secure it with the support stay, inserting the stay into its slot on the bonnet.

To close: put the stay back in its clip, lower the bonnet to about 30 cm above the latch and let it drop. Check that it is properly latched.

> **WARNING:** Make sure the engine is switched off before working under the bonnet. The cooling fan can start at any time even with the engine stopped.

> **WARNING:** Engine parts can be very hot after driving. Wait until the engine has cooled before touching anything.

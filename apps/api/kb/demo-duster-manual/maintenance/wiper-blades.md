Replace the wiper blades when they leave streaks, judder or make noise — typically once a year.

To replace a front blade, switch off the ignition, lift the wiper arm away from the windscreen, release the clip holding the blade and slide it off the arm. Fit the new blade until it clicks, then lower the arm gently onto the glass.

> **WARNING:** Do not let a wiper arm without a blade spring back onto the windscreen; it can crack the glass.

> **NOTE:** In frosty weather, check that the blades are not frozen to the glass before switching on the wipers.

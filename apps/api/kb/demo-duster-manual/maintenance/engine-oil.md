Check the oil level regularly, for example every time you refuel and before a long journey. It is normal for an engine to use some oil between oil changes.

1. Park on level ground and switch off the engine. Wait a few minutes for the oil to drain back into the sump.
2. Open the bonnet and pull out the dipstick (it usually has a coloured handle).
3. Wipe it with a clean cloth, push it fully back in, then pull it out again.
4. The level must be between the minimum and maximum marks.

If the level is near or below the minimum, top up through the filler cap with the oil grade specified in your official manual or service record. Add a small quantity at a time and recheck; do not go above the maximum mark.

> **WARNING:** Never top up while the engine is hot, and never let oil drip onto hot engine parts.

> **WARNING:** If the oil pressure warning light comes on while driving, stop as soon as it is safe and switch off the engine. Driving on can destroy the engine.

> **NOTE:** Take used oil to a recycling point; never pour it into drains.

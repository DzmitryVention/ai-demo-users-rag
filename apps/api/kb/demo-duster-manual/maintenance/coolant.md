The coolant protects the engine against overheating and freezing.

Check the level with the engine **cold** and the vehicle on level ground. The level in the expansion tank must be between the minimum and maximum marks. If it is low, top up with the coolant type specified in the official manual.

If you need to top up frequently, there may be a leak: have the cooling system checked by a workshop.

> **WARNING:** Never open the expansion tank cap while the engine is hot. The coolant is under pressure and can cause severe burns.

> **WARNING:** If the temperature warning light comes on, stop as soon as it is safe, switch off the engine and let it cool. Do not continue driving.

> **NOTE:** Coolant is toxic. Keep it away from children and animals.

The 12 V battery is maintenance-free: you do not need to top up the electrolyte. Its charge can drop when:

- the vehicle is used mainly for short trips;
- it stands unused for several weeks;
- many electrical consumers are used with the engine off;
- temperatures are very low.

If the vehicle will not be used for a long time, have the battery checked beforehand or use a suitable maintenance charger.

Signs of a weak battery are a slow-turning starter motor, dim lights at start-up, or electrical warning messages. Have the battery tested by a workshop.

> **WARNING:** The battery contains sulphuric acid and produces explosive gases. Keep flames and sparks away, wear eye protection, and avoid contact with skin and eyes.

> **WARNING:** Disconnecting or replacing the battery can affect electronic systems. Have it done by a qualified workshop.

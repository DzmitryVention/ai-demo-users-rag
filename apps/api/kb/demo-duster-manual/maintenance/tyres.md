**Pressures.** Check the tyre pressures at least once a month and before long journeys, with cold tyres. The correct pressures for your vehicle are shown on the label on the driver's door frame and in the official manual. Increase pressures when the vehicle is fully loaded, as indicated on the label.

**Tread.** Check the tread depth regularly. The legal minimum depends on your country; many tyres have wear indicators in the grooves that become flush with the tread when the tyre must be replaced.

**Condition.** Look for cuts, bulges or objects stuck in the tread. Replace tyres in pairs on the same axle with the size and type recommended for your vehicle.

**Tyre pressure monitoring.** If a tyre pressure warning appears, check and adjust the pressures as soon as possible.

> **WARNING:** Under-inflated or damaged tyres can overheat and burst, and they increase braking distances. Never drive on a visibly damaged tyre.

> **NOTE:** Winter tyres improve grip in cold, snowy and icy conditions; local rules may require them.

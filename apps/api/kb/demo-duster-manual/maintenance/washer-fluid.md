The washer fluid reservoir is under the bonnet, identified by a windscreen symbol. Some versions show a low-level message on the instrument panel.

Top up with a ready-mixed screen wash. In winter, use a product with antifreeze protection suitable for the lowest temperatures you expect.

> **NOTE:** Do not use plain water in freezing conditions: it can freeze in the pipes and damage the pump.

> **NOTE:** Avoid using the washers when the reservoir is empty; the pump can be damaged by running dry.

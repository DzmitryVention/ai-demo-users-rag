Wash the vehicle regularly, especially in winter when roads are salted, and remove bird droppings, insects and tree sap quickly because they damage the paint.

- Use car shampoo and plenty of water; rinse thoroughly.
- In a car wash, fold the door mirrors and remove any aerial or roof accessories.
- With a pressure washer, keep the nozzle at a distance from seals, sensors, the camera and the engine compartment.
- Clean the interior with a damp cloth and mild soap; do not use solvents on plastics or upholstery.

> **WARNING:** Never wash the engine compartment with a high-pressure jet: electrical components can be damaged.

> **NOTE:** Keep the parking sensors, camera lens and lights clean for safety.

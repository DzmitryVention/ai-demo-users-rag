Regular servicing keeps the vehicle safe and reliable and protects your warranty.

Service intervals depend on distance and time — whichever comes first — and on the engine type. The instrument panel reminds you when a service is due. The detailed schedule is in the service record supplied with your vehicle.

A typical service includes an engine oil and filter change, checks of fluids, brakes, tyres, lights and steering, and replacement of filters and wear parts at the specified intervals.

Keep all invoices and have each service recorded. This is useful for warranty claims and when you sell the vehicle.

> **NOTE:** Vehicles used mostly for short trips, towing, dusty roads or extreme temperatures may need more frequent servicing. Ask your workshop.

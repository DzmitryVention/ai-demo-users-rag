The brake fluid reservoir is under the bonnet, marked with a brake symbol. The level must be between the minimum and maximum marks.

The level drops slightly as the brake pads wear; this is normal. A sudden or large drop may indicate a leak.

Brake fluid absorbs moisture over time and must be replaced at the intervals given in the service schedule.

> **WARNING:** If the brake fluid level is below the minimum, or the brake warning light comes on while driving, stop as soon as it is safe and contact a workshop or roadside assistance. Do not drive the vehicle.

> **WARNING:** Brake fluid is corrosive and damages paint. Any work on the braking system must be carried out by a qualified workshop.

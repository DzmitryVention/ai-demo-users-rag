Depending on the version, your vehicle comes with a remote-control key or a hands-free key card. Both lock and unlock the doors and allow the engine to be started.

**Battery in the remote.** If the range of the remote becomes shorter or it only works close to the vehicle, its battery is probably weak. Most remotes have a backup method to unlock the door mechanically and to start the engine; see your official manual for the exact procedure for your key type.

**Spare and lost keys.** Keep the spare key at home, never inside the vehicle. If a key is lost or stolen, contact an authorised workshop: lost keys can be deactivated and new ones programmed to the vehicle.

> **WARNING:** Never leave children or animals alone in the vehicle with the key inside. They could start the engine, operate the windows or release the parking brake.

> **NOTE:** Dispose of used key batteries at a collection point, not in household waste.

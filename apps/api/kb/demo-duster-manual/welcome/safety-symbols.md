Two kinds of highlighted boxes are used throughout this guide.

A **WARNING** describes a situation where there is a risk of injury to you or other people, or of serious damage to the vehicle. Read warnings before carrying out the procedure they belong to, and follow them every time.

A **NOTE** gives useful extra information: a tip that makes a task easier, a reminder about the environment, or a hint to avoid minor damage.

When a procedure says that work must be done by a qualified workshop, do not attempt it yourself. Modern vehicles contain high-pressure, high-temperature and electrical systems that require special tools and training.

> **WARNING:** Never ignore a warning box because a task seems simple. Many injuries happen during routine jobs such as opening the bonnet with the engine hot or lifting the vehicle on a jack.

Your vehicle has a unique 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN). You will need it when ordering parts, booking a service, or contacting support about a recall.

The VIN usually appears in several places:

- on the registration document;
- on a plate visible through the bottom of the windscreen on the driver's side;
- on the manufacturer's plate on a door pillar or under the bonnet.

The paint code, the permitted weights and other identification data are printed on the manufacturer's plate described in the technical chapter.

> **NOTE:** Never share your VIN, registration number or key details in public places. When contacting support, give them only through the official channels.

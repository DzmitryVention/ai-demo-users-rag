This demo guide explains everyday use and basic care of your Renault Duster in plain language. It is organised in seven chapters: getting to know the controls, driving, comfort, maintenance, practical advice for problems on the road, and technical information.

Equipment differs between versions and markets. If a feature described here is not fitted to your vehicle, simply skip that page. Where exact figures matter — tyre pressures, fluid grades, maximum loads — this guide tells you where to find the official value on your vehicle rather than quoting numbers.

Keep the official owner's manual and the service record in the vehicle. If you sell the car, hand them over to the next owner.

> **NOTE:** This is demo content for a help-desk demonstration. For anything safety-related, always check your vehicle's official owner's manual or ask an authorised workshop.

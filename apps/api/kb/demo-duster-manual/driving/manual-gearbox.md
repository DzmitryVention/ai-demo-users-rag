The gear pattern is shown on the gear lever knob.

- Always press the clutch pedal fully when changing gear.
- Select reverse only when the vehicle is completely stationary. On many versions you must lift a ring under the knob to engage reverse.
- A gear-shift indicator on the instrument panel may suggest when to change up or down to save fuel.

Do not rest your foot on the clutch pedal while driving and do not hold the vehicle on a slope using the clutch; both wear the clutch quickly.

> **WARNING:** Do not coast downhill in neutral. You lose engine braking and may have less control of the vehicle.

> **NOTE:** If gears become hard to select or the clutch pedal feels different, have the vehicle checked by a workshop.

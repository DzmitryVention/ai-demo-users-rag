Depending on the version, the parking brake is a lever between the front seats or an electronic switch.

**Lever.** Pull the lever up firmly to apply it. To release, pull it up slightly, press the button at the end and lower the lever fully.

**Electronic parking brake.** It may apply automatically when you switch off the engine and release automatically when you drive off with the seat belt fastened. It can also be applied and released with its switch.

The brake warning light stays on while the parking brake is applied. If it stays on after releasing the brake, see the warning-lights page.

> **WARNING:** On a slope, turn the front wheels towards the kerb and engage a gear (or P) in addition to applying the parking brake.

> **WARNING:** Never drive with the parking brake partly applied: the brakes can overheat and lose efficiency.

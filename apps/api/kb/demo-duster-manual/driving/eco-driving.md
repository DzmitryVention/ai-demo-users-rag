Your driving style has a large effect on fuel consumption.

- Drive smoothly: anticipate traffic and avoid hard acceleration and braking.
- Change up early, following the gear-shift indicator where fitted.
- Keep a steady speed; higher speeds increase consumption significantly.
- Switch off air conditioning when it is not needed and keep the windows closed at high speed.
- Remove roof bars and boxes when they are not in use.
- Do not carry unnecessary weight.
- Check tyre pressures regularly: under-inflated tyres increase consumption and wear.

The trip computer shows the average consumption, which helps you see the effect of these habits.

> **NOTE:** Short trips with a cold engine use much more fuel. Combine errands into one journey when you can.

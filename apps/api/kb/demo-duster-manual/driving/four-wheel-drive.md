Four-wheel-drive versions have a mode selector. Typical modes are an automatic mode for most conditions, a locked mode for low-grip surfaces at low speed, and a two-wheel-drive mode for normal roads to save fuel. See your official manual for the modes available on your version.

**Off-road tips:**

- Walk the route first if you are unsure about depth, slope or ground firmness.
- Drive slowly and steadily; avoid sudden acceleration or steering.
- Climb and descend slopes straight on, never across.
- Use hill descent control, where fitted, on steep descents.
- After off-road use, check the underbody and tyres for damage and clean off mud.

> **WARNING:** Never drive through water whose depth you do not know. Water entering the engine air intake can cause serious engine damage.

> **WARNING:** Use the locked four-wheel-drive mode only on low-grip surfaces and at low speed; it can make the vehicle difficult to steer on dry roads.

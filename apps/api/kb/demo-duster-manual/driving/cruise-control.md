The **cruise control** keeps a set speed without pressing the accelerator. The **speed limiter** prevents you from exceeding a set speed while you control the accelerator yourself. Both are operated with buttons on the steering wheel.

To use cruise control: switch the function on, reach the desired speed and press the "set" button. Adjust the speed with the + and − buttons. Pressing the brake or clutch suspends the function; the "resume" button returns to the stored speed.

> **WARNING:** Do not use cruise control in dense traffic, on winding or slippery roads, or in poor visibility. You remain responsible for your speed and distance at all times.

> **NOTE:** On steep descents the vehicle may exceed the speed set on the limiter; brake if necessary.

Depending on the version, the vehicle is fitted with rear and/or front parking sensors and a rear-view camera.

The sensors measure the distance to obstacles while manoeuvring. A beep becomes faster as you approach an obstacle and becomes continuous when it is very close. The camera image appears on the multimedia screen when reverse gear is engaged, with guide lines showing the vehicle's path.

The system cannot detect every obstacle: narrow posts, low kerbs, chains or objects above the bumpers may not be detected.

> **WARNING:** Parking aids do not replace your attention. Always check around the vehicle, especially for children and animals, before and during manoeuvres.

> **NOTE:** Keep the sensors and camera lens clean and free of snow, ice and mud for the system to work correctly.

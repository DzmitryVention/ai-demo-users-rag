Before towing, check that your version is approved for towing and that the tow bar is fitted and connected according to the manufacturer's instructions.

The maximum braked and unbraked trailer weights and the maximum load on the tow ball are given in the vehicle's registration document and on the manufacturer's plate. Never exceed them.

- Distribute the load in the trailer with the heaviest items over the axle.
- Check the trailer lights and tyre pressures before setting off.
- Drive at reduced speed, allow longer braking distances and take bends gently.
- On long climbs in hot weather, watch the engine temperature.

> **WARNING:** Exceeding the permitted towing weights or the tow-ball load affects braking and stability and can cause an accident.

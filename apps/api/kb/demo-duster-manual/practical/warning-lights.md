Warning lights use colours to show how urgent a problem is:

- **Red** — stop as soon as it is safe to do so, switch off the engine and contact a workshop or roadside assistance. Examples: oil pressure, engine temperature, brake system, charging fault.
- **Orange / amber** — have the vehicle checked as soon as possible. Examples: engine management fault, airbag fault, tyre pressure, ABS fault.
- **Green or blue** — information only, for example lights or cruise control switched on.

Most lights come on briefly when you switch on the ignition as a self-test and then go out. A light that stays on or comes on while driving needs attention.

A **flashing** engine management light usually indicates a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter: reduce speed and have the vehicle checked immediately.

> **WARNING:** If a red warning light comes on while driving, do not continue your journey. Stop safely and get help.

> **NOTE:** When contacting support about a warning light, describe its colour and symbol and any message shown on the display.

Before winter, have the battery tested, check the coolant protection and fill the washer reservoir with a winter screen wash. Consider winter tyres where conditions or local rules require them.

- Clear snow and ice from all windows, lights, mirrors and the roof before setting off.
- Drive smoothly, reduce speed and increase your following distance.
- Use snow chains only on the wheels and in the way indicated in the official manual, and only on snow-covered roads.
- Carry a scraper, a blanket, warm clothes and a charged phone on long winter trips.

> **WARNING:** If the vehicle is stuck in snow with the engine running, make sure the exhaust pipe is clear of snow to prevent exhaust gases entering the cabin.

> **NOTE:** Do not apply the parking brake for long periods in severe frost if it may freeze; follow the advice in the official manual.

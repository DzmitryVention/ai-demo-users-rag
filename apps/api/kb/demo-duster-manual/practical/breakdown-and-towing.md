In case of a breakdown:

1. Stop in a safe place, as far from traffic as possible, and switch on the hazard warning lights.
2. Put on a high-visibility vest before getting out and make all passengers leave the vehicle by the side away from traffic.
3. Place the warning triangle according to local rules.
4. Call your roadside assistance service. Have your location, registration number and a description of the problem ready.

**Being towed.** Use the towing eye supplied with the vehicle, screwed into its socket in the bumper. The ignition must be on so that steering and brake lights work; steering and braking will require much more effort without the engine running. Four-wheel-drive and automatic versions may need to be transported on a flatbed — check the official manual.

> **WARNING:** On motorways, wait behind the safety barrier, never on the hard shoulder or in the vehicle.

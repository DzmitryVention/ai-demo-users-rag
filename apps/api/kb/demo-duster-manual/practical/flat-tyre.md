Depending on the version, the vehicle carries a spare wheel with a jack, or a tyre repair kit.

**First steps:** stop in a safe place away from traffic, switch on the hazard warning lights, apply the parking brake, ask passengers to leave the vehicle and stand in a safe area, and wear a high-visibility vest. Place the warning triangle according to local rules.

**Repair kit.** The kit can temporarily seal small punctures in the tread. Follow the instructions on the kit, then drive at reduced speed to a tyre specialist. It cannot repair cuts in the sidewall or large punctures.

**Changing the wheel.** Follow the official manual for jack positions and procedure. If you are not confident, call roadside assistance.

> **WARNING:** Never get under a vehicle supported only by a jack, and use the jack only on firm, level ground at the jacking points.

> **WARNING:** A temporary spare wheel or a repaired tyre is for limited distance and speed only. Have the tyre repaired or replaced as soon as possible.

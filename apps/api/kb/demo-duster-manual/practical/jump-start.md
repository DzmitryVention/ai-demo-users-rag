If the battery is flat, the engine can be started using another vehicle's battery of the same voltage (12 V) and jump leads.

1. Make sure the two vehicles do not touch. Switch off the ignition and all electrical consumers in both.
2. Connect the red lead to the positive (+) terminal of the flat battery, then to the positive (+) terminal of the booster battery.
3. Connect the black lead to the negative (−) terminal of the booster battery, then to an unpainted metal earth point on the engine of the vehicle that will not start — not to the flat battery itself.
4. Start the booster vehicle's engine and let it run for a few minutes, then start your engine.
5. Disconnect the leads in the reverse order.

If the engine does not start after a few attempts, stop and call roadside assistance.

> **WARNING:** The battery can release explosive gas. Keep flames and sparks away, do not lean over the battery and never let the clamps touch each other.

> **NOTE:** After a jump-start, drive for at least 30 minutes so the battery can recharge, and have it tested if it goes flat again.
